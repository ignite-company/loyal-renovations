Every project has its own requirements. These stages explain the questions and decisions that help a renovation move forward.

02

QUESTIONS WORTH ASKING.

What is the must-have?

Distinguish the things your renovation needs to solve from the additions that would simply be nice to include. This helps guide important scope choices.

What is behind the walls?

Existing conditions can affect renovation work. Older spaces or substantial changes may call for investigation before timelines and budgets are finalized.

How do we use the space?

Talk through traffic flow, storage, lighting, accessibility, family routines, and how the room should function over time.

What does success look like?

Photos and inspiration are helpful, but so is being specific about comfort, maintenance, durability, and what a better everyday experience means to you.
