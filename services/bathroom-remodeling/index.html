RETHINKING YOUR BATHROOM

A bathroom renovation can make a routine feel entirely different. Whether the goal is easier mornings, better organization, a more relaxing shower, or an updated look, careful planning makes the biggest difference.

Bathrooms are compact rooms with a lot happening beneath the surface. Plumbing, moisture management, ventilation, lighting, layout, and finish choices all affect how well the space performs. We begin with your needs before talking about fixtures or tiles.

Showers, tubs, and layouts

Discuss the space available, accessibility, the desired bathing experience, and whether an existing layout can support your priorities.

Tile, surfaces, and fixtures

The materials you choose should complement the style of the space while standing up to water, cleaning, and daily wear.

Storage, lighting, and ventilation

Vanities, mirrors, task lighting, and good air movement can make a small bathroom feel more comfortable and practical.

Planning considerations

Before work begins, it helps to talk through a few essentials:

  • Who uses the bathroom, and how often?
  • Do you want to keep your current fixtures in place or rethink the layout?
  • Is moisture, ventilation, or storage a current concern?
  • Which finishes will be practical for long-term upkeep?

Bathroom timelines and cost can shift with waterproofing needs, plumbing conditions, tile complexity, material availability, and municipal requirements.
